# Formal star-gate borders

The six gold metal frames from `机制切图01/方块边框金属边缘` are transparent
overlays for star gates. They sit above the matching colour gate and below its
counter/ice number, preserving the gate colour while making the star route
visually distinct.

Horizontal and vertical variants are supplied for one, two and three cells;
longer gates are split with the same native 1/2/3-cell policy as ordinary
formal gates.
